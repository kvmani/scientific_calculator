SCIENTIFIC GUIDE

Expression evaluation

The service parses an expression into a syntax tree, admits only numeric operations and approved functions, binds finite variables and an explicit angle convention, then evaluates one point or a bounded grid.

Algorithm

  1. Normalize ^ to exponentiation.
  2. Parse without executing Python source.
  3. Reject attributes, imports, keywords, and unapproved nodes.
  4. Bind constants and variables.
  5. Evaluate and reject complex or non-finite results.

Main equations

\[ x_{k} \;=\; x_{0} + k\,\Delta x, \qquad k = 0,1,\dots,N-1 \]

A plot evaluates the same validated expression on a bounded uniform grid.

\[ N \;=\; \left\lfloor \frac{x_{\text{stop}} - x_{0}}{\Delta x} \right\rfloor + 1 \]

The sample count sets plot resolution and is checked against the 5,000-point limit.

\[ \sin_{\deg}(\alpha) \;=\; \sin\!\left(\frac{\pi\,\alpha}{180}\right) \]

Degree mode converts trigonometric inputs before applying the standard radian functions.

where
\(x_{0}\)start of the plotted range
\(\Delta x\)positive step size
\(N\)number of sampled points, capped by the point limit
\(\alpha\)angle supplied in degrees

Critical inputs

Expression
Defines operators, functions, constants, and variables.
Variables
Must bind every non-constant name to a finite number.
Angle unit
Controls trigonometric inputs and inverse-trigonometric outputs.
Range and step
Control sampling density and must remain below 5,000 points.

Limits

  • Numerical floating-point evaluation is not symbolic algebra.
  • Use Unit Converter when dimensions or physical units matter.
  • A curve visualizes the sampled formula; it does not validate a physical model.

Privacy

This service runs on the office intranet. Expressions are evaluated in memory for the duration of your request, nothing is stored, and no personal information is collected.
